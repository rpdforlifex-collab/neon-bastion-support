ACCOUNT & DATA DELETION

Delete your Neon Bastion account.

In the game

  1. Open Settings → Cloud Save.
  2. Check that the selected account is the one you want to delete.
  3. Choose Delete Account and follow the confirmation.
  4. Keep the app installed and connected until Cloud Save shows reset complete.

This clears local progress and requests deletion of the selected cloud account. If offline, the request remains queued until the game reconnects. Uninstalling alone does not request cloud deletion.

Without the game

You can request deletion by emailing the developer. Use the subject “Neon Bastion account deletion” and include your account ID, if available. We will explain how to verify ownership before deleting the account. Do not send a recovery code or password in your first email.

Request account deletion

You can also copy the address: rpdforlifex@gmail.com.

What is deleted

The selected account’s cloud save, recovery mapping, and device memberships are removed. Its unshared anonymous identities and associated game event records are removed too.

What may remain

Other cloud accounts and the identities or records they still need are preserved. Other devices may retain local copies. Registered identities used by other services are preserved.

Deleting active records does not immediately erase historical service logs or disaster-recovery backups. Our current Supabase Pro configuration provides seven-day project-log retention and a rolling seven days of daily database backups. These copies age out separately. Provider-held security/legal records may have separate retention obligations; the seven-day setting is not a guarantee covering every provider-held record.
