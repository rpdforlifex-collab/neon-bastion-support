Before you begin
Use a compatible Android device and a US Google Play account. Use the same Google account for the group, the testing invitation, and your Play Store. Open this page in Chrome if an Instagram or other in-app browser gives you trouble.

Complete these steps in order. Google handles sign-in; this page never asks for your password. Each button opens a new tab so you can come back here.

  1. Join the tester group

    Open Neon Bastion Testers, sign in with your Play Store Google account, and select Join group. Complete Google's join confirmation. Already a member? Continue to step 2.

    1. Join the Google Group ↗

    Group membership gives you eligibility. It does not enroll you in the Play test yet.

  2. Become a tester

    Open the invitation below using that same Google account and select Become a tester. Continue when Google says You are a tester.

    2. Become a tester ↗

  3. Install and play

    Once enrolled, follow Download it on Google Play on the invitation page, or use this button. Then tap Install.

    3. Install on Google Play ↗

    Please stay in the group and opted in for at least 14 consecutive days, play regularly, and share honest feedback. This is a pre-release build; features may change.

Something not opening?

“App not available” or “Item not found”

First complete group membership, then become a tester. Opening the install link alone does not grant access. Check the Google account in your browser AND the Play Store profile menu; they must match. Confirm your Play account's country is United States. Newly granted access may take time to appear—retry later if the accounts and membership are correct. Do not uninstall an existing game or clear its data to fix enrollment.

I cannot find “Join group”

Sign in to Google in Chrome, then reopen step 1. On a narrow screen, check the group's About page or its menu. If Google says you are already a member, go to step 2. If you still cannot join, email support with a screenshot of the message; hide private account details first.

I'm using an iPhone or a non-US Play account

This invitation is for the US Android closed test only. It does not install an iPhone app. Contact support to ask about future availability; do not change your Play country just to join.

Do I need to pay or share a password?

The game download is free. No purchase is required to participate. Do not assume any in-game purchase is a no-charge test; only buy if you intend to pay. Never send us passwords, verification codes, or payment details.

Tell us how it went
