Email support

Before you write

Include your device model, app version, and what happened. For a save problem, include your account ID if available. Do not send passwords, recovery codes, or payment details in your first message.

Back up or restore progress

Open Settings → Cloud Save → Cloud Privacy and choose Enable Cloud. You can also choose Play Locally to stop cloud and online-event requests without deleting your progress. Keep your recovery code private: anyone who has it can access your backup. Cloud backup needs an internet connection.

Your data

Read the privacy policy or delete your account and associated game data.
