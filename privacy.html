Privacy Policy — Neon Bastion: Idle Defense

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Neon Bastion: Idle Defense ("the app") is developed by Roger Proenza ("we", "us"). This policy describes the data used by local and cloud game saves.

The short version

The app saves gameplay progress on your device. Cloud backup and online-event requests are off until you choose Enable Cloud in Settings > Cloud Save > Cloud Privacy. Choosing Play Locally keeps them off; ordinary gameplay remains available. Older installations also need to make this choice, without losing their existing local progress or protected recovery credentials. If you enable cloud, the app sends a copy of your progress to Supabase for backup and recovery. Cloud save currently uses a randomly assigned, pseudonymous account ID; it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, advertising ID, or GPS location. Our cloud provider processes network information, including IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, as described below. We do not use advertising analytics, behavioral profiling, or cross-app tracking. We use technical service logs to troubleshoot and secure the cloud service. The current release configuration does not offer a real-money purchase.

Advertising and purchases

The current version does not show third-party advertising or offer in-app purchases, and it does not collect advertising or purchase information. If we introduce either feature, we will update this policy and the app store privacy disclosures before releasing it.

Data stored on your device

The app stores game progress and preferences in the platform's app storage, including upgrade levels, coins, gems, tier and wave progress, research, cards, powers, audio/visual settings, save version, and timestamps needed for offline earnings. The configured build also stores a Supabase session and a high-entropy recovery code after enabling its cloud-save connection. On iOS, those two credentials are stored as Keychain items that are available only when the device is unlocked and are restricted to that device. On Android, they are encrypted with authenticated AES-GCM using an app-owned key held by Android Keystore; the encrypted records remain in the app's private storage. The app does not fall back to ordinary preferences if protected credential storage is unavailable. Existing plaintext credentials from an older build are deleted only after the upgraded app verifies a protected copy.

On iOS, the operating system may include local app data in an iCloud device backup according to your device settings and Apple's policies; the protected cloud credentials described above are marked as device-only and do not migrate to another device through that backup. The Android app deliberately disables operating-system app-data backup. Cloud transfer and recovery use only the in-app Cloud Save controls described below.

Cloud-save data

After you enable cloud, when the app is online or later reconnects, it may transmit the following over encrypted HTTPS connections:

  • the gameplay and settings save data described above;
  • a random Supabase user ID used only to keep each player's row separate;
  • save version, lifetime coin total, and last-backup time, used to resolve save conflicts safely;
  • a one-way SHA-256 hash of the recovery code. The readable recovery code is shown to you and is not stored in the database;
  • when online events are available, event participation, reward claims, tournament attempts/results, and security/operation records associated with the random user ID, used to run events and prevent duplicate rewards or abuse;
  • network and technical information processed by Supabase and its infrastructure providers, including IP addresses, approximate IP-derived country or region, request paths, account identifiers, timestamps, response status, software/user-agent information, and service timing information. These service logs support authentication, troubleshooting, reliability, rate limiting, security, and abuse prevention. The app does not request GPS/location permission.

This data is used for app functionality, account/recovery operations, technical diagnostics, security, and fraud or abuse prevention. It is not used for advertising, marketing, behavioral personalization, or cross-app tracking.

Service provider

Supabase hosts the authentication and database infrastructure as our service provider. Supabase processes cloud-save data on our behalf and under our configuration. We do not sell cloud-save data or share it with advertising networks or data brokers.

The current game database is hosted in the United States (Oregon). Network infrastructure and support providers may process information in other locations under their applicable terms and safeguards.

Retention, recovery, and deletion

Cloud progress remains until you delete it or the service is retired. The random recovery code lets you transfer a backup after reinstalling or changing devices; anyone who obtains that code could restore the backup, so keep it private.

Settings > Cloud Save > Cloud Privacy > Play Locally stops further cloud and online-event requests. This keeps your local progress, existing server backup, and protected recovery credentials. It does not delete your account. Requests already received by the server cannot be recalled. You can re-enable cloud later; existing save-conflict checks still apply.

Settings > Cloud Save > Delete Account deletes local progress and queues deletion of the selected cloud account, its save, recovery mapping, and device memberships. When a device identity is no longer attached to any cloud account, its game event records are removed and its anonymous authentication identity is retired. An identity that is still used by another cloud account, and records needed for that other account, remain until those accounts are also deleted. Reset does not delete other accounts you previously switched to or copies still stored on other devices. Registered identities used for other services are not deleted by the game's account-reset operation.

If the device is offline, server deletion stays queued until the app can connect; keep the app installed and reopen it online to finish. The Cloud Save screen shows whether reset is still queued or complete. Uninstalling by itself does not request server deletion. An explicitly requested deletion can connect and authenticate while cloud is otherwise off, solely to finish deleting the selected account. Resetting a local-only profile without a cloud identity does not create an account. After a completed reset, automatic backup stays paused until you explicitly enable cloud and choose backup or restore again. Deleting active game records does not immediately erase historical service logs or disaster-recovery backups. Our current Supabase Pro configuration provides seven-day project-log retention and a rolling seven days of daily database backups. These copies age out separately from the live database. Provider-held security/legal records may be subject to the provider's own retention obligations; the seven-day setting is not a promise that every provider-held record is erased within seven days.

To request deletion without using the app, email rpdforlifex@gmail.com with the subject "Neon Bastion account deletion" and your account ID, if available. We will explain how to verify ownership before deleting an account. Do not include a recovery code, password, or payment details in your first message. You may also use this address for privacy questions.

Age and audience

The app is intended for players aged 13 and older, not for children under 13. The data practices above also apply to teenage players. A content age rating is not a promise that no personal data is processed. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data through the cloud service, contact us so we can investigate and arrange deletion after verifying the request. A parent or guardian may also use the in-app account-deletion controls.

App store platforms

Apple App Store and Google Play may independently process install statistics, crash reports, purchases, or device-backup data under their own privacy policies. Those platform activities are separate from the app's Supabase cloud save.

Support website

Our public support, privacy, and account-deletion pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. They do not contain an account sign-in, an upload form, advertising, or analytics scripts added by us. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, including when a visitor is not signed in to GitHub. That hosting activity is governed by GitHub's Privacy Statement and is separate from the game's cloud-save data. Email links open your mail app; you choose whether to send a message and what information to include.

Changes

We will update this policy before enabling materially different data uses, such as behavioral analytics, third-party advertising, or real-money purchases, and will update the app store privacy disclosures at the same time.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: rpdforlifex@gmail.com

If you email us, we receive your sender address and the information you choose to include. We use that information to respond, troubleshoot, and verify account-deletion requests. The in-app Email Support button opens your mail app; it does not silently upload your save or send a message. Please do not send passwords, recovery codes, or payment details in your first message.
